# Knowledge-source inventory

Load authorized, current copies of the sources below into the NeuralSeek KnowledgeBase. Keep the legal text in the KnowledgeBase rather than committing restricted or copyrighted copies to this repository.

Every record must carry:

- `tier` (an integer from 1 to 5);
- source title and issuing body;
- publication or effective date and version/repeal status;
- provision/article or page reference; and
- the exact excerpt used for an answer.

## Tier 1 — primary national law

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Law No. 90-11 of 21 April 1990 on labor relations | Binding labor-relations provisions |
| Ordinance No. 97-03 on the legal duration of work | Working-time and related provisions |

## Tier 2 — institutional guidance

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Ministry of Labor, Employment and Social Security (MTESS) publications and guidance | Official explanations and administrative guidance |

Record the publication date and official URL for every guidance document. Guidance cannot override Tier 1.

## Tier 3 — implementing instruments

Add applicable executive decrees and other implementing regulations, each with its issuing authority, effective date, and provision reference. Do not use this tier for a rule that conflicts with a directly applicable Tier 1 provision.

## Tier 4 — collective agreements

Add only registered or otherwise verifiable sectoral or collective agreements. Record the sector, parties, coverage, effective period, and the clauses relevant to the question. These records cannot establish an entitlement contrary to mandatory higher-tier law.

## Tier 5 — local and secondary context

Add company policies, internal procedures, and clearly identified secondary explanations only as context. They may explain how an organization operates, but cannot establish a national legal entitlement or override Tiers 1–4.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The record is current or explicitly marked superseded.
- [ ] The tier is supported by the source type.
- [ ] The jurisdiction and worker population are clear.
- [ ] The citation points to an article, section, or page.
- [ ] Conflicting records are retained and dated, not silently deleted.
- [ ] Restricted documents are stored only in an authorized KnowledgeBase.
